# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of contributors' time. Discuss
the behavior or code, not the person. Russian and English are both welcome.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of personal
information, and spam are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, or other
project spaces. Do not publish someone else's credentials or private logs.

Maintainers may edit or remove offending contributions, warn participants,
lock discussions, or restrict participation. Use GitHub's private abuse-reporting
tools to report harmful behavior without repeating personal information publicly.
These rules apply to maintainers and contributors alike.
